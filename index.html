HACKNIGHT 1.0

MMU · Feb 6–7, 2026

Hacknight 1.0 — first of its kind at MMU

Beginner-friendly CTFs across web exploitation, stego, network forensics, crypto, OSINT, and misc — hosted on Cyber Sensei’s platform. Sixty-plus students filled the room.

Leads stayed on the floor all night: mixing gurus with beginners, freezing the scoreboard at 4:30am, then running creator walkthroughs until sunrise. Writeups went out on Medium. Merch and VIP memberships for the winners.

  • Custom CTF platform + challenge authoring
  • Balanced teams so beginners learn mid-competition
  • Refreshments, awards, and dawn photo finish
  • Immediate walkthroughs — not “figure it out later”

Hacknight 2.0 — Beyond the Code

Thu 28 May 2026 Korea Lab, MMU 7:00pm – 6:00am

Sold-out second edition. Bigger attendance, electric energy from industry gurus to beginners, and a night of nonstop ethical hacking with live guided walkthroughs from the challenge creators — Cyber Sensei-EH.

Partners who showed up with us: Hackviser (exam vouchers & VIP subs), Hack The Box Kenya Meetup (awards), PerfectRoot (CTF platform & servers), HackerSavanna, Moringa School, System 32, and Cyber Chief Circle in the room.

  • Balanced CTF challenges authored by Cyber Sensei
  • Mentorship sessions for people in the journey
  • Late-night technical deep-dives into the early morning
  • 2am bug-bounty session with steiner254

GUEST Hacknight 2.0 · 2:00am

Industry gurus in the room.

We brought in steiner254 — a top bug-bounty hunter in the region who leads the team at hackersavanna.com — for a deep-dive bug bounty session at 2am.

That is the bar: not only challenges, but operators who ship results in the wild — sharing tradecraft while students are still in the fight.
