01 About

Operators who test, teach, and ship security work.

Cyber Sensei is a cybersecurity practice built by people who live in the work — breaking applications, stress-testing platforms, training teams, and mentoring the next wave of practitioners.

We move between offense and defense: finding weaknesses, explaining them clearly, and helping people and organizations get stronger.

  • Offensive & defensive security
  • Hands-on delivery
  • Built in public on YouTube
